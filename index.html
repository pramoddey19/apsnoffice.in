CAPEX vs OPEX: Which Office Model Is Right for You?

A traditional rented property may look cheaper when you compare only the monthly rent. The real decision should include fit-out, furniture, IT, deposits, utilities, facility management, maintenance and the cost of capital tied up before the office is operational.

Cost / Business Factor Traditional Rented Office Managed Office / OPEX Model
Initial CAPEX High — fit-out, furniture, electrical, IT and other setup costs are normally funded by the company. Low to minimal — the workspace is delivered ready to use, depending on the operator and agreement.
Security Deposit Usually a significant upfront cash commitment. Often lower than a traditional lease, but varies by provider and commercial terms.
Furniture & Fit-out Company arranges, pays and manages it. Generally included in the managed-office service.
IT / Internet / Utilities Usually separate contracts and ongoing management. Often bundled into one monthly fee; verify inclusions and usage limits.
Facilities & Maintenance Company manages vendors, housekeeping, security and maintenance. Operator typically manages day-to-day facilities.
Time to Occupy Can take months when fit-out and approvals are required. Typically faster because the space is already operational.
Scalability Changing the footprint can mean renegotiating or taking another property. Often easier to add or reduce seats, subject to availability and contract terms.
Cash-Flow Profile Large upfront CAPEX + recurring operating costs. More of the cost can move into predictable recurring OPEX.
Best Fit Businesses needing maximum control, customisation and a long, stable occupancy horizon. Growing teams prioritising speed, flexibility and preservation of capital.

🏗️ Traditional Lease = Capital + Operations

Your rent is only one part of the cost. Before comparing proposals, calculate fit-out CAPEX, furniture, IT, deposit, CAM, electricity, maintenance, staffing and exit/restore costs.

📊 Managed Office = Predictable OPEX

A managed office can convert much of the office setup and operating burden into a recurring service cost, helping businesses preserve capital for growth and reduce setup complexity.

Important: This is a decision framework, not a claim that managed offices are always cheaper. The best option depends on rent, fit-out cost, team size, contract term, utilisation, escalation, deposit, service inclusions and your company's required level of control.
