Workspace economics

CAPEX vs OPEX: which office model fits your business?

Compare the full cost and operating effort—not just the monthly rent—before choosing a traditional lease or managed office.

Business factorTraditional rented officeManaged office / OPEX model
Initial investmentFit-out, furniture, electrical and IT setup are generally funded by the company.Often lower upfront setup burden, depending on the operator and agreement.
DepositCan involve a significant upfront cash commitment.Varies by provider, duration and commercial terms.
Furniture & fit-outCompany typically arranges and manages it.Often included as part of the managed workspace offering.
IT & utilitiesMay require separate vendors, contracts and management.May be bundled; verify bandwidth, usage limits and inclusions.
FacilitiesCompany coordinates housekeeping, security and maintenance.Operator typically manages day-to-day facilities.
FlexibilityChanges to footprint may require a new lease or property negotiation.Seat or space changes may be easier, subject to availability and contract terms.
Best suited forBusinesses prioritising control, customisation and longer occupancy.Teams prioritising speed, flexibility and reduced setup complexity.

Important: A managed office is not automatically cheaper. Compare total occupancy cost, deposit, escalation, service inclusions, taxes, contract term, utilisation and exit conditions using actual proposals.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you begin

Common questions about managed offices and workspace discovery in Pune.

What is a managed office?

A managed office is a professionally operated workspace that may include ready-to-use furniture, internet, meeting facilities and day-to-day facility management. Inclusions depend on the operator and agreement.

Can APSN help find private cabins or team offices?

Yes. APSN helps businesses explore private cabins, managed offices, dedicated desks and team workspaces based on requirements.

Which areas of Pune do you cover?

We support workspace discovery across major Pune business districts including Hinjewadi, Baner, Balewadi, Wakad, Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Kalyani Nagar, Hadapsar and Magarpatta, subject to current availability.

Can we visit a workspace before deciding?

Yes. Once suitable options are shortlisted, APSN can assist with coordinating site visits.

How do I get current pricing?

Pricing depends on location, seat count, facilities, contract duration and current availability. Share your requirements so suitable proposals can be explored.

Does APSN provide employee relocation support?

APSN can help employees explore accommodation and neighbourhood options as part of relocation coordination. Property availability, verification and final agreements are handled directly with the relevant providers or owners.

How do I contact APSN?

Email info.apsnconsultancy@gmail.com or use the workspace enquiry form. You can also connect through our LinkedIn company page.
